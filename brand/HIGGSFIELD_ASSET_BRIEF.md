# AgentDesk — Higgsfield asset brief

Use GPT Image 2 for all four assets so the product UI, typography, and social identity stay in one visual language.

## Art direction

Orbital mission control. Deep graphite-black, luminous mint (`#7DFFD5`), soft bone (`#EFF9F4`), and very sparse warm coral (`#FF715B`). Precision instrument rather than cyberpunk game. Fine technical lines, atmospheric depth, restrained glow, dense black negative space, and crisp geometric surfaces. Avoid purple, blue neon, glossy humanoid robots, stock 3D characters, and generic circuit-board imagery.

## 1. Twitter / X profile image

Output target: `web/public/brand/generated/x-profile.png`, square.

Prompt:

> A premium square brand avatar for an AI agent orchestration product named AgentDesk. A single custom geometric capital A symbol built from three precise luminous mint planes, centered on a near-black graphite field. The symbol feels like an orbital command beacon, with one tiny warm coral status light and an extremely subtle radial instrument grid. Bold silhouette, readable at 32 pixels, clean edges, no words, no letters besides the abstract A mark, no mockup, no border, no robot, modern Swiss technical identity, high-end software brand, tack sharp.

Output target: `web/public/brand/generated/x-banner.png`, generate wide and crop to 1500 × 500.

Luminous mint, bone white, one tiny coral alert, no purple, no blue neon, no humanoid robots, no fake browser chrome, premium futuristic software identity, extremely crisp.
